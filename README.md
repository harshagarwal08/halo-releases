# Halo

A tiny menu bar app for macOS: hold ⌥ and drag files to convert, shrink, crop, merge and share them. Everything happens on your Mac.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/harshagarwal08/halo-releases/releases/latest)**: open the DMG and drag Halo to Applications.

Halo checks here once a day and offers to update itself.

Requires macOS 14 or later (voice commands need macOS 26).
